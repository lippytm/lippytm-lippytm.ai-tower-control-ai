# Legal Documentation Control Manual

**Owner and final authority:** Charles Earl Lipshay

## Purpose

This manual governs the continuing documentation of the lippytm.AI Project of Projects, Business of Businesses, System of Systems, AI Agnostic Clone Engine Fabric, Prompt #11, Gemini and NotebookLM work, GitHub repositories, films, music, publishing, robotics, blockchain, Linux, wearable security, legal claims, historical research, advertising, and future-technology development.

The documentation system is a continuing work in progress. Every material idea, asset, event, decision, communication, expense, revenue item, allegation, source, publication, advertisement, AI output, and project change should be recorded in a form that can later support legal review, valuation, licensing, historical preservation, business planning, and public communications.

## Governing Principles

1. Preserve originals and work from copies.
2. Separate verified facts, witness statements, reports, opinions, theories, fiction, satire, and future scenarios.
3. Record who created, reviewed, approved, changed, published, or distributed each item.
4. Never overwrite a historically important record without retaining the prior version.
5. Keep confidential legal, medical, financial, identity, security, and witness information out of public repositories.
6. Use consistent IDs, dates, versions, ownership fields, confidentiality levels, and review statuses.
7. Treat AI-generated material as a draft until human-reviewed.
8. Do not claim legal conclusions, valuations, scientific proof, partnerships, endorsements, or investment returns without competent support.
9. Maintain redundant backups and periodically test restoration.
10. Connect public advertising claims to approved source records.

## Evidence Preservation Procedure

1. Preserve the original file or message unchanged.
2. Record where and how it was obtained.
3. Generate a checksum when practical.
4. Store a protected archival copy.
5. Create a separate working copy.
6. Record every material transformation or annotation.
7. Do not delete unfavorable or contradictory material.
8. Preserve context, metadata, attachments, and related communications.
9. Restrict access according to sensitivity.
10. Obtain attorney guidance before public disclosure of sensitive evidence.

## Confidentiality Levels

### Public
Approved for websites, repositories, advertisements, and public distribution.

### Internal
Business operations and development records not intended for general publication.

### Confidential
Sensitive financial, identity, partner, unpublished IP, or security information.

### Restricted Legal
Potential claims, witness material, legal strategy, attorney communications, and evidence requiring controlled access.

### Restricted Medical and Identity
Medical records, Social Security information, government IDs, banking details, passwords, private keys, and authentication data.

## Public Repository Prohibition

Never store the following in a public repository:

- Passwords or authentication codes
- Private keys or wallet seed phrases
- Full Social Security numbers
- Unredacted government IDs
- Banking and card numbers
- Medical records
- Confidential witness identities
- Attorney-client communications
- Private legal strategy
- Exact security vulnerabilities affecting current systems
- Home location or continuous personal-location data

## Advertising Evidence Rule

Every material advertising claim should link internally to one of the following:

- Completed product
- Demonstrable feature
- Reliable source
- Customer evidence with permission
- Qualified forecast clearly labeled as a forecast
- Approved founder statement clearly labeled as vision or opinion

Never advertise speculation as established fact or an unregistered valuation as a guaranteed market value.
