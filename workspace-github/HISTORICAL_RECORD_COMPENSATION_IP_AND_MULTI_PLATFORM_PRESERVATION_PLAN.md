## Compensation and Valuation Workstream
A multi-million-dollar value cannot be established solely from hours worked or the number of ideas. Build an evidence-backed valuation using:
- documented labor and replacement cost
- completed assets and source files
- ownership and contributor records
- prototypes and technical feasibility
- audience, customers, sales, licensing, and recurring revenue
- comparable transactions and market evidence
- projected cash flow with explicit assumptions
- trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, domains, repositories, data, and brand value
- litigation or damages only when supported by counsel and evidence

Required deliverables:
- Master Asset Registry
- Project-by-Project Valuation Matrix
- Founder Contribution Ledger
- Commercialization Readiness Scores
- Comparable-Company and Comparable-License Analysis
- Independent valuation by a qualified valuation professional
- Attorney-reviewed ownership and chain-of-title report

## Copyright and IP Workstream
For every asset record:
- asset ID, title, creator, creation date, version, source file, owner, contributors, licenses, publication history, confidentiality level, registration status, infringement evidence, and commercial uses

Separate:
- copyrightable expression
- trademarks and brand identifiers
- patentable inventions
- trade secrets
- contracts and licenses
- public-domain and third-party material
- ideas and concepts that are not protected by copyright alone

Use written contributor agreements and assignments. Do not publish confidential invention details before patent counsel reviews them. Preserve suspected infringement with dated copies, URLs, screenshots, source files, comparison tables, and evidence of access; do not make public accusations before legal review.

## ChatGPT Preservation Workstream
- Request a personal ChatGPT data export through ChatGPT Settings > Data Controls > Export where eligible, or through the OpenAI Privacy Portal.
- ChatGPT Business workspace export is not available through the ordinary settings export; maintain a separate project-by-project archival process for Business workspace material.
- Save important conversations as PDF, HTML, plain text, and structured summaries.
- Record conversation title, date range, account/workspace, project, participants, export method, source URL or identifier, confidentiality, and checksum.
- Preserve uploaded and generated files separately.
- Never rely on one ChatGPT workspace as the only copy.

## Gemini and NotebookLM Preservation Workstream
- Use Google Takeout for available Google-account data.
- Export or copy critical notebook source documents, notes, generated reports, audio overviews, and metadata into controlled project folders.
- Maintain a 100-notebook registry containing notebook ID, title, purpose, owner, sources, last update, confidentiality, IP status, and GitHub reference.
- Do not assume every NotebookLM artifact will appear in Takeout; verify each notebook and preserve critical outputs separately.

## GitHub Preservation Workstream
- Maintain canonical repositories plus encrypted offline mirrors.
- Clone repositories with full history, tags, branches, issues/PR exports where needed, releases, wikis, actions artifacts, and dependency records.
- Use signed commits or documented author identity where practical.
- Add PROJECT_MANIFEST, IP_REGISTER, RISK_REGISTER, SECURITY_NOTES, REVENUE_PLAN, LEGAL_REVIEW_QUEUE, and SYNC_STATUS to priority repositories.
- Keep confidential medical, financial, identity, legal-strategy, witness, password, private-key, and security records out of public repositories.

## 3-2-1-1-0 Preservation Rule
- 3 copies of every critical record
- 2 different storage media
- 1 off-site copy
- 1 offline or immutable copy
- 0 unverified backup errors

Recommended copy classes:
1. Working copy
2. Encrypted local archive
3. Encrypted off-site archive
4. Periodic read-only snapshot
